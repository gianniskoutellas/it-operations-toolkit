# IT Operations Toolkit

A small portfolio of practical templates and notes for IT Business & Operations roles.

## Purpose

This repository demonstrates practical understanding of:

- Google Workspace administration workflows
- CRM and ATS concepts
- IT asset, vendor, procurement, and facilities coordination
- Low-code automation for request intake and notifications
- IT support documentation and escalation practices

## Contents

- `google-workspace/` — administration runbook and user lifecycle checklists
- `crm-ats/` — CRM and ATS concepts, data-quality checklist
- `procurement-facilities/` — asset inventory, vendor register, purchase workflow, maintenance log
- `automation/` — low-code request-intake automation design

## Scope and limitations

This is a learning and portfolio project. It does not represent production
administration, live business systems, or real company data.

All examples use synthetic, non-sensitive information. No credentials,
personal data, or confidential business information are included.

## How to use

Review the Markdown files for workflows, checklists, and operational templates.
The CSV files are examples of simple operational registers that can be adapted
for a small organisation.
