# Purchase Request Workflow

## Purpose
Standardise requests for equipment, supplies, software, and external services.

## Workflow
1. Employee submits a request with business need, item, quantity, and deadline.
2. Manager reviews the business need and budget.
3. Operations checks existing inventory, contracts, and approved suppliers.
4. Approval is obtained according to the spending threshold.
5. Purchase is made through the approved supplier.
6. Invoice, delivery, and asset details are recorded.
7. Asset is tagged and added to the inventory.
8. Request is closed with confirmation from the requester.

## Required fields
- Requester
- Department
- Item or service
- Business justification
- Estimated cost
- Required-by date
- Approval status
- Supplier
- Invoice/reference number
- Asset ID, if applicable

## Controls
- No purchase without approval.
- Use approved suppliers where available.
- Keep invoices and delivery records.
- Update the asset inventory after delivery.
- Review recurring subscriptions before renewal.
