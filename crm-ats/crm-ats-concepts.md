# CRM and ATS Concepts

## CRM
A Customer Relationship Management system stores and tracks relationships with customers, leads, partners, and suppliers.

Typical CRM data:
- Contact name and company
- Email and phone
- Owner/account manager
- Stage, e.g. new lead, contacted, qualified, closed
- Next action and follow-up date
- Notes and communication history

## ATS
An Applicant Tracking System manages the recruitment process.

Typical ATS stages:
1. Applied
2. Screening
3. Interview
4. Offer
5. Hired / Rejected

## Administration responsibilities
- Create and manage user accounts and permissions
- Maintain pipeline stages and custom fields
- Identify and resolve duplicate records
- Maintain accurate contact and company data
- Configure notifications and basic reporting
- Escalate integration or access issues to the vendor

## Data-quality checklist
- No duplicate contacts
- Required fields completed
- Correct owner assigned
- Clear next action and due date
- Sensitive documents stored according to policy
- Access reviewed when staff change roles or leave

## Scope
This is a conceptual learning document, not a record of production CRM/ATS administration.
